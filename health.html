Miso Sheetpan Chicken & Brussels 🌱
Yield: 4 servings | Health Focus: Gut Microbiome & Anti-Inflammatory Fats

Why It's Healthy: White miso provides fermented probiotics supporting gut health, extra-virgin olive oil supplies stable monounsaturated fats, and Brussels sprouts deliver high doses of sulforaphane and cellular-protective antioxidants.

Ingredients
  • ⅓ cup white miso & ⅓ cup maple syrup
  • ⅓ cup extra-virgin olive oil & 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 12 oz Brussels sprouts (trimmed and halved)
  • 3 to 4 chicken breasts (~2 lbs, cubed into 1 ½-inch pieces)
Preparation
  1. Step 1: Heat oven to 425°F. Line a baking sheet with aluminum foil.
  2. Step 2: Whisk miso, maple syrup, olive oil, and cumin in a bowl. Reserve a scant ¼ cup (3 tbsp) of mixture for later. Toss chicken and sprouts in the remaining marinade.
  3. Step 3: Spread everything in a single layer on the baking sheet, scraping remaining marinade on top.
  4. Step 4: Bake 8 mins. Remove, daub reserved 3 tbsp marinade on top, and bake 4 mins more.
  5. Step 5: Switch to broil for 1–2 mins until chicken is cooked through and slightly charred. Serve warm.
Substitutions: Chicken can be swapped for steak or salmon. Brussels sprouts can be swapped for green beans or mushrooms.
Nutrient-Dense Broth & Ramen 🍜
Yield: 2–3 servings | Health Focus: Collagen, Minerals & Aromatics

Why It's Healthy: Slow-simmered bone broth is rich in joint-supporting collagen and bioavailable trace minerals, while fresh ginger and garlic deliver potent immune-boosting and anti-inflammatory properties.

Ingredients
  • Rich bone broth base
  • Crushed garlic, fresh ginger, and scallions
  • Sliced mushrooms and leafy greens
  • Seared steak or shrimp, soft-boiled egg, dash of sesame oil
Preparation
  1. Step 1: Simmer bone broth base with crushed garlic, fresh ginger, and scallions.
  2. Step 2: Incorporate sliced mushrooms and leafy greens to maximize micronutrient profile.
  3. Step 3: Finish with seared steak or shrimp, soft-boiled egg, and a dash of sesame oil.
Substitutions: Easily adaptable with tofu or chicken based on preferred protein sources.
