Step-by-Step Custom Recipes:

Sheet-Pan Chicken & Sprouts 🌱

Focus: High-heat roasting with healthy fats and unrefined glaze.

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Toss Brussels sprouts and chicken cuts in extra virgin olive oil.
  2. Season generously with sea salt, cumin, and cracked black pepper.
  3. Roast for 25 minutes until caramelized, drizzle lightly with pure maple syrup in the final 3 minutes, and serve.
Nutrient-Dense Broth & Ramen 🍜

Focus: Slow-simmered remakes with fresh aromatics and clean proteins.

  1. Simmer a rich bone broth base with crushed garlic, fresh ginger, and scallions.
  2. Incorporate sliced mushrooms and leafy greens to maximize micronutrient profile.
  3. Finish with seared steak or shrimp, soft-boiled egg, and a dash of sesame oil.
