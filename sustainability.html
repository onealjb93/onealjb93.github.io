Nature as Model, Measure, and Mentor

From biologist Janine Benyus's 1997 book Biomimicry: Innovation Inspired by Nature, advanced by the non-profit Biomimicry Institute: learn from 3.8 billion years of evolution — ask how nature solves it, then emulate the strategy. The AskNature database catalogues those strategies. biomimicry.org ↗

A Regenerative Economic System

From the Ellen MacArthur Foundation (founded 2010): an economic system that eliminates waste and pollution, circulates products and materials at their highest value, and regenerates nature — mapped in the butterfly diagram of technical and biological cycles. ellenmacarthurfoundation.org ↗
