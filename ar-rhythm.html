Threads

How to play

Threads has two modes — Build and Play — switched with the Build | Play control at the bottom of the stage. Build is where strings are made and removed. Play only plays: nothing you do there can add a string or take one away.

  1. Start — in Build, pinch your thumb and index finger together.
  2. Drag — keep pinching and move your hand. The thread follows your fingertip and stretches.
  3. Drop — open your fingers wide. The string stays pinned.

Remove a string in Build — pinch it and hold it still. The string thins as it lets go; when it fades, it is gone. Move your hand and it stays. Undo brings a removed string back.

In Play — pinch on a string to sound it, drag while pinched to keep it ringing, and let go to stop it. Pinching empty space does nothing. An open hand crossing the strings stays silent. Short strings sing high, long strings sing low.

Pick an instrument at the top — Guitar, Violin, Cello or Harp — and every new string you place takes that instrument's voice and colour. Strings already placed keep theirs.

Add drums with the Drums button — Kick, Snare, Hi-hat and Hand drum. Tap a drum to play it, sweep across it to strike it. Pinch a drum with two fingers to size it: bigger drums sound deeper and louder, smaller drums higher and softer.

Record your voice with the Record button and it loops under everything you play. In Tools, Save setup keeps your web, your drums and your loop on this device only, ready next visit — nothing is uploaded.

Prop your phone still — a steady camera keeps everything exactly where you put it, and frees both hands.

Where it plays best

Great — bright, even light on your hands; a plain background behind them; a table, counter, or shelf to prop the phone at arm's length; a still room, with nothing moving behind you. Art likes a clear wall; Cloth likes a table edge.

Rough — dim or lamplit rooms; a window behind you; busy, cluttered backgrounds; mirrors and glossy reflections; harsh sun and glare outdoors; anywhere the phone can't sit still — walking, or in a car.

Cloth

One piece of silk, put down in front of you. Your fingertip presses it; a pinch catches a handful and pulls. Hold a handful still to pin it there; let go and it drops — and stays where it lands. Bring your hand closer and the cloth comes with it. Set a table edge from the buttons below the stage and the cloth drapes over it.

Art

Pinch to paint in the air. Your strokes hang in space where you draw them. Place a wall frame from the tools below and the paint lives inside it — on the wall, in the wall's perspective.

How to paint
  1. Paint — pinch your thumb and index finger together and move. The brush follows your fingertip.
  2. Lift — open your fingers wide. The stroke ends and stays where you drew it.
  3. Tools — pick a brush (Paint, Light, Ribbon), a color, and a size below. New settings apply to the next stroke.

Strokes float on a plane in front of the camera. In touch mode, drag to paint and drag with two fingers to look around them.
