Threads
  1. Start — pinch your thumb and index finger together. A dot appears.
  2. Drag — keep pinching and move your hand. The thread follows your fingertip and stretches.
  3. Drop — open your fingers wide. The string stays pinned.

Then play — sweep an open fingertip across the strings to sound them. Short strings sing high, long strings sing low.
