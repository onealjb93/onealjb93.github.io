💡 Peter Lynch's Growth at a Reasonable Price (GARP)

Peter Lynch filtered out overpriced traps and unviable businesses using four primary gates:

1. PEG Ratio (≤ 1.0) A PEG under 1.0 indicates a bargain relative to earnings growth.
2. Earnings Growth (20%–50%) Targeted fast growers in the 20–25% sweet spot. Rates above 50% are traps.
3. Debt-to-Equity (≤ 0.30) Low debt ensures stability because "companies without debt can't go bankrupt."
4. Institutional Ownership (< 50%) Prefers lower Wall Street backing so the "hidden gem" phase isn't over.

🪄 Joel Greenblatt's Magic Formula

Combines value and quality systematically to remove human emotion and bias:

1. Earnings Yield (Cheapness) Operating income / Enterprise value. Ranks companies on cash generation relative to price tag.
2. Return on Capital (Quality) Pre-tax operating profit / (Net fixed assets + Working capital). Measures asset efficiency.
3. Combined Score Sum of EY rank + ROC rank. Lowest combined scores represent top buy candidates.
4. Portfolio Discipline Buy top 20–30 stocks, hold for 1 year, sell, and repeat mechanically.
