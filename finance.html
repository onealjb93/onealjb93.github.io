High-Conviction Watchlist

Apple Inc. AAPL ↗

Designs, manufactures, and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables, and accessories, alongside a rapidly scaling services ecosystem.

⚡ Why It's Promising
Unrivaled customer loyalty, massive high-margin service revenue compounding, and an exceptionally pristine balance sheet.
Microsoft Corp. MSFT ↗

Global technology leader providing enterprise productivity software, cloud computing infrastructure (Azure), artificial intelligence platforms, and gaming.

⚡ Why It's Promising
Mission-critical enterprise software stickiness combined with aggressive leadership in cloud infrastructure and enterprise AI integration.
NVIDIA Corp. NVDA ↗

Pioneered GPU-accelerated computing, powering the global artificial intelligence revolution, deep learning models, data centers, and advanced visualization.

⚡ Why It's Promising
An insurmountable hardware and CUDA software moat driving the fundamental compute infrastructure for the entire global AI buildout.
Alphabet Inc. GOOGL ↗

Dominant digital advertising engine, search monopoly, cloud provider (Google Cloud), and pioneer in artificial intelligence research (DeepMind).

⚡ Why It's Promising
Deep proprietary data moats across search and video (YouTube), paired with world-class AI research and exceptional free cash flow generation.
