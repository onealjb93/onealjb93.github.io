💡 Peter Lynch's Growth at a Reasonable Price (GARP)

Peter Lynch filtered out overpriced traps and unviable businesses using four primary gates:

1. PEG Ratio (≤ 1.0) A PEG under 1.0 indicates a bargain relative to how fast earnings are compounding.
2. Earnings Growth (20% – 50%) Lynch targeted fast growers in the 20–25% sweet spot. Rates above 50% are unsustainable traps.
3. Debt-to-Equity (≤ 0.30) Low debt ensures stability because "companies without debt can't go bankrupt."
4. Institutional Ownership (< 50%) Prefers lower Wall Street backing so the "hidden gem" phase isn't already over.
