💡 Peter Lynch's GARP Filtering Framework

Legendary fund manager Peter Lynch pioneered Growth at a Reasonable Price (GARP) to filter out overpriced traps and unviable businesses. Activating the filter below evaluates companies against his four primary gates:

1. PEG Ratio (≤ 1.0) Compares valuation (P/E) to growth. A PEG under 1.0 indicates a bargain relative to how fast earnings are compounding.
2. Earnings Growth (20% – 50%) Lynch targeted fast growers in the 20–25% sweet spot. Rates above 50% are viewed as unsustainable traps.
3. Debt-to-Equity (Low / ≤ 0.30) Lynch favored low or zero debt because "companies without debt can't go bankrupt," safeguarding against downturns.
4. Institutional Ownership (< 50%) Prefers lower Wall Street backing. High institutional ownership means the "hidden gem" phase is already over.
