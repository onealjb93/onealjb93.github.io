Line Rider

Draw a track, then send the rider down it. Downhill he picks up speed, off a ramp he flies — steer him in the air with the arrow keys and land him back on the track. If his head hits a line, he crashes. How far can you keep him going?

How to play

  1. Draw a track — drag on the canvas with your mouse or finger. Downhill slopes build speed; ramps launch him into the air.
  2. Move the view — tap ✋ Move, then drag to slide the window around without drawing anything. Zoom with the − / + buttons, scroll on the canvas, or pinch on your phone; "Fit track" shows the entire track at once. The view stays exactly where you leave it — it never snaps back.
  3. Press Play (or Space) — the rider sets off from the flag. While he's airborne, ← and → spin him. Land on your wheels, not your head.
  4. Don't crash — if his head hits a line, he's done. Pause (Space) to draw more track, then Play to keep going.
  5. Tools — Erase wipes lines, Clear all starts fresh, Default track brings back the starter track, Restart (R) sends him back to the flag. Your best distance is saved on your device.
