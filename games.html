Line Rider

Draw a track, then send the rider down it. Downhill he picks up speed, off a ramp he flies — steer him in the air with the arrow keys and land him back on the track. If his head hits a line, he crashes. How far can you keep him going?

Distance0.0 m

Best0.0 m
Draw a track, then press Play.

How to play: drag on the canvas to draw track — downhill for speed, ramps for air. Press Play (or Space) to ride, Pause (or Space) to stop and draw more, Restart (or R) to go back to the start. While airborne, ← and → spin him — land on your wheels, not your head. Works with mouse or touch.
